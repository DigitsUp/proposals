List building from zero

Every buyer ends up on the list

Before launch, the waitlist is the product. After launch, every order from the site or TikTok Shop is a chance to capture a subscriber you own.

01

Waitlist + site capture

ANTI builds the page, we wire it: two-step pop-up (email, then SMS), embedded waitlist block, exit intent.

02

VIP by text

SMS subscribers shop every drop 24 hours early. That’s the reason to hand over a number.

03

Register-your-pair card

A QR insert in every order for fit help, care tips and first access. We build the offer and flow; ANTI designs the card.

04

Text-to-join keywords

A keyword and flow for each creator. Your TikTok team hands them out; we report who grows the list.

05

Lead forms, synced

Your performance team runs the ads. We connect lead forms to Klaviyo so every lead lands tagged and gets the welcome flow.

We don’t run TikTok, creators, social or paid. We share CRM data and segments with those teams whenever it helps.
